# Using the Open Timegrapher name and logo

Open Timegrapher is free software. The name and logo say that something
*is* Open Timegrapher, or *is about* it. This policy keeps that true, and
otherwise gets out of the way.

## You may, without asking

- Use the name and logo to refer to Open Timegrapher: in an article, a
  video, a review, a course, a talk, a forum post or a list of tools.
- Use them in a link or a button that leads to the project or its
  download page.
- Say that your product, add-on, stand or microphone "works with Open
  Timegrapher", if it does.
- Package an official release under its own name and icon, for example in
  a Linux distribution, with changes only for packaging.
- Make merchandise for yourself or for a club, not for sale.

## Please don't

- Call a modified build or a fork "Open Timegrapher", or give it this logo.
  Give it its own name and icon. "Based on Open Timegrapher" in its
  description is welcome.
- Use the name or logo in the name of your own product, company or
  domain.
- Suggest that the project endorses you, your product or your service
  when it hasn't.
- Change the logo (see "Don't" in [GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md#dont)), except to
  scale it or to use one of the one-colour versions.

If you're not sure, open an issue and ask. The answer is usually yes.

policy is about the name and logo as marks, which that licence leaves
out.
